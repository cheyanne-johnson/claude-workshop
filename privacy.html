1. Who we are

This site, its waitlist, and the workshops and events it promotes (including the Claude Workshop for Maxed Out Founders, the Claude & Notion Workshop, and AI Coworking sessions) are run by Cheyanne Johnson. For the purposes of data protection law, Cheyanne Johnson is the "data controller" — the person responsible for deciding what personal data is collected and why.

Contact: hello@cheyannejohnson.com

2. What we collect

We keep this to the minimum needed to run the waitlist, the workshops, and our email communications:

  • Name — so we can address you properly and check you in at events.
  • Email address — to send you updates, event details, and (once you've opted in) general email list content.
  • What you told us you're interested in — e.g. the workshop, the Claude & Notion Workshop, or AI Coworking, so we send relevant updates instead of everything to everyone.
  • Where you came from — a short tag (like "instagram" or "newsletter") showing which link you used to reach the waitlist, so we know what's working. This is not linked to any advertising profile or third-party tracker.
  • Basic technical data — standard web server logs (like IP address and timestamp) that our hosting provider, Vercel, collects automatically for security and reliability. We don't actively use this to identify you.

We do not collect payment details, government ID numbers, or any special category data (health, religion, etc.) through this site.

3. How we collect it

Directly from you — when you fill out the waitlist form, register for a workshop, reply to an email, or otherwise contact us. We don't buy, scrape, or acquire your data from third parties.

4. Why we use it, and our legal basis

Under GDPR, we need a valid legal reason ("legal basis") for everything we do with your data. Here's the breakdown:

PurposeLegal basis
Sending you waitlist updates and details about the events you selectedConsent — given when you check the box on the waitlist form
Adding you to our general email list for occasional updatesConsent — given as part of the same checkbox, explained clearly before you submit
Running the workshop itself (logistics, check-in, follow-up)Performance of a contract, once you register for a paid or free event
Keeping the site and form secure and working (basic server logs)Legitimate interest in running a functioning, secure website

You can withdraw consent at any time — see Your rights below.

5. Our email list, specifically

When you join the waitlist and check the consent box, you're doing two things at once, and we want to be upfront about both:

  • You're asking to hear about the specific events you checked (the workshop, the Claude & Notion Workshop, and/or AI Coworking).
  • You're also being added to Cheyanne's main email list. As of this policy's last update, that list isn't actively being used yet — but when it is, you'll be folded in and may receive occasional updates beyond just workshop logistics.

Either way: no spam, no selling your address, and you can unsubscribe or ask to be fully removed at any time — see Your rights.

6. Who we share it with

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers. We do use a small number of service providers ("processors") to actually run the site and, eventually, send emails:

  • Vercel — hosts this website and stores waitlist signups.
  • Substack — this waitlist is expected to migrate here for ongoing email communication. When that happens, this policy will be updated, and your data will only move over for the purposes already described above.

These providers only process data on our instructions and aren't allowed to use it for their own purposes.

7. International data transfers

Our hosting provider (Vercel) operates infrastructure in the United States and other countries outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Where your data is transferred outside the EEA or UK, it's done under the provider's standard contractual safeguards recognized under GDPR. If you'd like more detail on these safeguards, contact us using the details below.

8. How long we keep it

We keep waitlist and event data until the relevant events (the workshop, the Claude & Notion Workshop, and AI Coworking sessions) have wrapped up, or until you ask us to delete it — whichever comes first. If you're on the general email list, we keep your details until you unsubscribe, ask us to delete your data, or after a period of prolonged inactivity, whichever comes first.

9. Cookies and tracking

This site does not currently use cookies, analytics scripts, or advertising trackers. If that changes — for example, if we add analytics to understand site traffic — this policy will be updated first, and where required, we'll ask for your consent.

10. Your rights

If GDPR applies to you (generally, if you're in the EU, EEA, or UK), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict or object to how we use your data
  • Port your data to another provider, in a common format
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting anything done before the withdrawal
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority — in France, this is the CNIL

These same principles apply to everyone who contacts us, regardless of where you're located — we're happy to honor these requests for any visitor. To exercise any of these rights, just email us — see Contact us.

11. How we protect it

Your data is stored on Vercel's infrastructure, which includes encryption in transit and standard access controls. Access to your data is limited to Cheyanne Johnson. No online system is 100% risk-free, but we don't collect more than we need, and we don't keep it longer than necessary.

12. Children's privacy

This site and its events are intended for adults running or working in businesses. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a minor has submitted their information to us, contact us and we'll delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

If how we handle your data changes in a meaningful way — like adding analytics, or migrating the waitlist to Substack — we'll update this page and change the "last updated" date at the top. For significant changes affecting how your data already collected is used, we'll aim to notify you directly by email.
